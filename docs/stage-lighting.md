# Stage lighting pass

The existing lights now give each part of the stage a clearer role:

- A warm, softer spotlight follows the tilted fretboard surface.
- Warm and cool side lights, plus a light behind the pit, pick out crowd bodies.
- The broad wash lights the backline rather than overlapping the fretboard key.
- Lower ambient fill keeps contrast between those areas.

Theme colors, musical reactions and the existing moving lights remain in use.
The pass adds no lights, shadow maps, geometry or post-processing effects.
